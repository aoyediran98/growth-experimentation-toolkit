How the maths works

CVR at a price = baseline CVR × (price ÷ baseline price)elasticity × (1 + framing CVR lift). Retention at a price = baseline retention × (1 + retention elasticity × price change), then × (1 + framing retention lift), capped at 99%. First-order RPV = CVR × first-order revenue. LTV per customer over the horizon = Σ monthly revenue × survival, where survival in month m is (1 − month-1 drop) × retentionm−1, and bundles pre-pay their months so survival is 1 for those months. Contribution LTV subtracts cost to serve per billed month. LTV per visitor = CVR × LTV per customer. Uncertainty: each lift and the elasticities are drawn from a normal with standard deviation = uncertainty × |value| ÷ 1.28, so the ± band is roughly P10–P90 on each input. Nothing here is a prediction; it is a way to see which assumptions a test result would have to overturn.

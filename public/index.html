Research to hypotheses

Paste messy inputs: session-recording notes, reviews, support-ticket themes, survey verbatims. They are clustered into problem statements, scored by frequency × severity, and mapped to a testable hypothesis each. Send any card to the roadmap or straight into a brief.

Page audit

Paste the visible copy of a landing page, or upload its HTML or screenshots, along with the ad that drives traffic to it if you have it. The page is scored against five fixed heuristics and each issue is mapped to a hypothesis in the same format as Synthesise.

HTML is read into the fields above. Images are sent to the model as pictures.

Fused insights

Combines the research synthesis (what customers say and do) with the page audit (what the page gets wrong). A problem that shows up in both is merged into one hypothesis and marked as corroborated; the rest carry over labelled by source.

Sample size and duration

Two-sided test of two proportions. Answers "can we even detect that?" before anything is built. Values here feed the brief and the peeking guard.

Run time by MDE at this traffic

Dotted line marks a 14-day floor: fewer than two full weekly cycles rarely gives a trustworthy read.

Test brief

Drafts the pre-commitment: metrics, success criteria, decision rule, pre-registered segments, sample and run time from the calculator, and a stop rule. The read-out judges results against this, so write it before the build.

The brief appears here.

Variants and persona pre-screen

Three copy variants per hypothesis for headline, subhead and CTA, each tagged with its principle and segment. The persona pre-screen ranks them using personas grounded in the research inputs. It is a prioritisation filter, never a substitute for the test. Link the variants to a test idea so they stay with its brief, then build the variant page from the control's HTML.

Build the variant page from the control

Upload the control page's HTML. Claude edits it to apply the hypothesis above, the brief (the linked idea's saved brief, or the one in the Brief tab) and the copy variant you pick. You get a change list and the edited page.

Roadmap

Every test idea, at whichever of the ten steps it has reached, with its ICE score and what has been saved to it. Open an idea to carry on where it stopped. "What next" orders the ideas not yet in build: riskiest assumptions first, cheap validations before big builds.

Peeking and early-stop guard

Enter where the test is today. The guard says whether a read is valid yet, simulates how often an A/A test would look "significant" if you peeked on your schedule, and gives the earliest valid read date.

False-positive rate when peeking on an A/A test

Monte Carlo, 2,000 simulated A/A tests at the current baseline and planned sample, calling the test whenever p < α at a peek.

Read-out

The conclusion mirrors the brief and closes the loop on the pre-committed decision rule. Statistics are computed here in the page; the AI only writes the narrative around numbers it is handed.

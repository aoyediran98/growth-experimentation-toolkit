Visual checks compare fonts, colours, images and alt text, headings, CTAs and viewport meta. Manual items persist in this browser per test name.

Review

Agree or clash

Same = identical across pages. Changed = differs and matches the scope. Clash = differs and is not in the scope, or a tracking or quality problem on one page only.

In the room

Business context

What are we trying to move, what can't change, and what is the business worried about. The facilitator edits; everyone can read.

Page under review

Imagery

Ads driving traffic and screenshots of the page. Used for message-match analysis in Insights.

Evidence

Anyone can add. Quant: paste a CSV or a table (funnel steps, segments, test history). Qual: verbatims, research notes, support themes. Opinion: a view from the room, logged as an opinion so it is weighed as one.

Insights

Each insight names its source so the room can challenge it. Add your own with the form, or edit any card.

Rough ideas

Half-formed is fine. Link an insight if one prompted it. Tick anonymous if you'd rather the room judged the idea, not the author; you can claim it later.

Board

Hypotheses

Ideas neatened into the standard form, duplicates merged, each tied to a metric and a surface. Edit anything; the author credit carries over.

Prioritisation

PXL-style: objective yes/no criteria the whole room can verify, an ease score, and the vote share. Weights are visible so nobody can quietly tilt the table. The facilitator can ask for AI-suggested criteria, then confirms each one.

Ranked

Roadmap

Sequenced by score, with one test per surface at a time, into monthly slots.

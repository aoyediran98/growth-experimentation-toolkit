Laws of UX Audit

Upload or paste a page's HTML and it is checked against the 30 Laws of UX: a deterministic pass that measures what can be measured (choices, targets, chunking, form length, speed proxies), then a reviewer pass that reads the page and judges the rest. Every finding names the law, the evidence and a testable fix.

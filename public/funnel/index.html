Funnel Diagnostic

Enter the steps and the users at each, by segment if you have it, and drop a screenshot on each stage. The funnel is drawn as a storyboard, the biggest leak is found and valued, and the diagnosis turns each leak into hypotheses in the standard form, ready for the roadmap.

Funnel

Segments:
Step conversion = users at this step ÷ users at the previous step. Value of a leak = the extra final conversions you would get by recovering 10% of the users lost at that step (they continue at the funnel's downstream rates), times value per conversion. Ranking by recoverable users, not by step rate, is what stops a small late step looking bigger than a huge early one. All computed in the page; the AI only interprets.
